# Privacy

Cash Compass 1.1.0 preview stores the plan you enter in this app's local WebView storage. It has no bank linking, account, analytics, advertising, AI API, or server connection. The Android app does not request internet permission. Its HTTPS asset origin is handled inside the app using bundled files; it is not an external website.

Backup text may contain personal financial details. It stays in the app until you copy it and choose where to save or share it. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes your plan. Android backup is disabled. Your device's own security and clipboard settings apply to local data.
